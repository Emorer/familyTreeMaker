Help & Guide

👤
Creating & editing cards
1
Click "New Person" in the sidebar
A form will appear where you can enter the person's name, surname, age, birthyear and place of birth.
2
Click "Create Person" to add the card
The card will appear on the canvas at position (0, 0). You can then drag it to any position.
3
Right-click a card to edit or delete it
A context menu appears with the options: Edit Card, Delete Card, Connect to Spouse and Connect to Parent.
🔗
Connecting family members
1
Spousal Connection
Select "Connect to Spouse" from the menu.
2
Select the Second Person
Click on the second person's card. A line will appear between the two cards, indicating their spousal relationship.
3
Add Child Connections
Parent–child relationships can be established in two ways:either select "Connect to Parents" and click on the spousal circle, or left-click on the spousal circle and select the child you want to link.
🗑️
Deleting Connections
1
Remove a Parent–Child Connection
Right-click on the connecting line between a parent and child to delete that specific connection.
2
Remove a Spousal Connection
Right-click on the spousal circle to delete the spousal connection along with all associated child connections.
